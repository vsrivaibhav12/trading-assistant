# Your day, in 3 steps

Everything else happens by itself.

## 1. Read your Telegram
- **☀️ Morning message** = the assistant is alive. It tells you the market mood. If it doesn't arrive on a weekday, see "If something stops working" in the setup guide.
- **🟢 BUY / 🔴 SELL message** = a trade idea. Most days there are none. When one comes it tells you, in plain words:
  - what to buy (or sell first) and around what price,
  - your **stop-loss** (the price where you leave to limit the loss) and how much that could cost you in rupees,
  - the **first and final targets**,
  - how much to buy,
  - why, and when to leave early.
- **🌙 Evening message** = a short summary of the day.
- **📅 Monthly stock list** = once a month, after the last trading day: which stocks to buy, sell or keep.

## 2. Tap a button
Under each idea there are two buttons:
- **✅ I bought it** → I'll watch the trade for you and tell you when to move your stop-loss or leave.
- **⏭ Skip this one** → I'll stop sending updates about it.

If you ignore a message, I keep sending updates in case you took it. Taps are read every 15 minutes, so a reply can take a little while. That's normal.

## 3. Do what the update says
| Message | What you do |
|---|---|
| 🎯 First target reached | Move your stop-loss to your buy price. You can sell half to lock in profit |
| ↗️ Moving your way | Move your stop-loss up to the new level |
| ⚠️ SELL now | Sell. The reason for the trade no longer holds |
| ⛔ Stop-loss reached | Sell if you haven't already |
| 🏁 Final target reached | Sell everything |
| ⏰ Time is up | Sell now (same-day trades end before the market closes) |

After a sell message, tap **✅ I've sold it** so your record is right.

**Always keep a stop-loss order with your broker** after you buy. My messages tell you where to move it.

## Once a week (optional, 2 minutes)
Open the webpage (the icon on your phone) → **📊 Is it working?** It shows which kinds of ideas are earning their keep:
🟢 proven · 🟡 promising · ⚪ too early · 🔴 not working.

## If you want a break
Webpage → **⚙️ Settings** → tick **Pause all new ideas**. I'll still warn you about any trade you're in.

## Real money rule of thumb
For the first 6 to 8 weeks treat every idea as practice. Only put real money behind ideas marked 🟢 or 🟡, and never more than the amount you set as your "most I'm willing to lose on one trade".
